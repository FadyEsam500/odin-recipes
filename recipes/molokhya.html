Molokhya

Description

Molokhya is a traditional Egyptian soup made from finely chopped jute leaves cooked in a rich, seasoned chicken or rabbit broth. It has a smooth, slightly silky texture and a distinct savory flavor.

The signature mark of a great Molokhya is the "Ta'la"—a sizzling mixture of crushed garlic and dry coriander fried in ghee, poured into the soup right before serving with warm pita bread or white rice.

Ingredients

  • 500g fresh or frozen Molokhya leaves, finely chopped
  • 4 cups chicken or rabbit broth
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp ground dry coriander
  • 2 tbsp ghee or butter
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Bring the broth to a gentle boil in a large pot over medium heat.
  2. Add the finely chopped Molokhya leaves to the hot broth and whisk thoroughly to dissolve all lumps.
  3. Simmer on low heat for 3 to 5 minutes without covering the pot to prevent the leaves from settling.
  4. Melt the ghee in a small frying pan over medium heat.
  5. Add minced garlic and dry coriander to the ghee, frying until golden brown and fragrant.
  6. Pour the sizzling garlic mixture (Ta'la) directly into the Molokhya pot and stir immediately.
  7. Remove from heat instantly and serve hot alongside white rice and toasted pita bread.
