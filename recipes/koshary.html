Koshary

Description

Koshary is Egypt's ultimate national street food, featuring a comforting mix of rice, brown lentils, and macaroni pasta. It is topped with crispy fried onions and served with a tangy garlic-vinegar sauce (Da'ah) and a spicy tomato sauce.

Originating as a budget-friendly staple in Cairo markets, it combines simple pantry ingredients into a flavorful, hearty meal that feeds a crowd.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Egyptian white rice
  • 1 cup brown lentils
  • 1 cup elbow macaroni
  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 cups tomato sauce with garlic and cumin
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced with vinegar and lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil, salt, and cumin

Steps

  1. Boil the brown lentils in salted water until tender, then drain and set aside.
  2. Cook the elbow macaroni according to package instructions, then drain.
  3. Cook the white rice with oil, salt, and cumin until fluffy.
  4. Deep-fry the sliced onions in hot oil until golden brown and crispy, then drain on paper towels.
  5. Simmer tomato sauce with garlic, cumin, vinegar, and salt in a saucepan for 10 minutes.
  6. Mix minced garlic, warm water, vinegar, lemon juice, and cumin in a jar to make the vinegar sauce (Da'ah).
  7. Layer rice, lentils, and macaroni in a bowl, top with tomato sauce and crispy onions, and drizzle with garlic vinegar sauce.
